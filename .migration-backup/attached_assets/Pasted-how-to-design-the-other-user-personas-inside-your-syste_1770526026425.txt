how to design the other user personas inside your system:

🧍‍♀️ 1️⃣ Nutritionist Persona – “Hormone Fuel Specialist”
🎯 Role

Improves:

Fertility outcomes

PCOS reversal

Pregnancy nutrition

Postpartum recovery

🖥 Dashboard Should Show

Patients assigned to nutrition care

Patient type tag: Fertility / Pregnant / Postpartum / PCOS

Latest weight trend

Hemoglobin / Vitamin D flags

Insulin resistance risk (for PCOS)

🛠 Key Features

Upload meal plans

Tag plans by goal: “Ovulation Support”, “GDM Diet”, “Postpartum Healing”

Track:

Weight changes

Bloating

Cravings

Sugar control logs

Adherence tracker (patient logs meals in app)

💡 Smart Alerts

“Low Hb + poor diet compliance”

“PCOS patient weight increasing for 2 cycles”

“Pregnancy patient inadequate weight gain”

Patients with high stress scores

“Fertility patient with 3 failed cycles + high stress”

🎯 Role

Manages patient movement & scheduling.

🖥 Dashboard

Today’s appointments

Patient type (Fertility / Pregnancy / etc.)

Next visit due alerts

Program enrollments (Saivie Care Plans)

💡 Alerts

“Fertility scan window tomorrow”

“Pregnancy scan due this week”

“Postpartum visit overdue”
