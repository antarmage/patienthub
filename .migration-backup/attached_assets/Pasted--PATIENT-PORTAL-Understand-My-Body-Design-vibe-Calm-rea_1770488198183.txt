🌸 PATIENT PORTAL — “Understand My Body”
Design vibe: Calm, reassuring, visual, non-technical Goal: Turn complex genomics + biology into clear life guidance

🏠 1. Home Screen — “Your Body Today”
First thing she sees when opening the app
Top Card (Dynamic)
“You’re in your Late Follicular Phase Estrogen rising • Energy building • Body preparing for ovulation”
Color shifts by phase (soft gradient system)
Below that: 3 Insight Cards
1️⃣ Body Mode
“Your metabolism is handling carbs efficiently today.”
2️⃣ Brain & Mood
“You may feel more social and mentally sharp.”
3️⃣ Reproductive Focus
“Fertility window approaching in ~3 days.”
CTA: “See Why” → opens science made simple

🔬 2. “My Biology” Tab (The Magic Layer)
This is where genomics + AI insights live.
🧬 Section: My Reproductive Blueprint
Simple language:
* “You may have a higher tendency for insulin resistance → lifestyle tips shown in luteal phase”
* “Your body may use antioxidants faster → ovulation support tips”
Each has: What this means → What you can do → When it matters in your cycle
No gene names upfront. Tap to expand for deeper science.

🌙 Section: Cycle Rhythm Map
A circular interactive wheel:
* Hormones
* Metabolism
* Brain mode
* Uterine lining
User slides across days → sees internal changes like a weather forecast for her body.

🥗 3. Daily Guidance Feed — “Aligned With You”
Instead of generic tips:
Today’s suggestions are phase + genome aware
Example:
🍳 Nutrition: Your body uses protein efficiently today. Add lentils, eggs, paneer. 🧘 Recovery: Magnesium may help with late-luteal tension. 💤 Sleep: Your temperature is rising — cool, dark sleep environment helps.
Feels like Spotify Discover Weekly for your biology

📈 4. Insights & Patterns
AI shows discoveries over time:
“You report anxiety mostly when progesterone is high.” “Your energy improves when sleep >7h in follicular phase.”
Visual correlations, not medical diagnosis.

🤰 5. Modes She Can Switch
* 🌿 General Cycle Health
* 🤍 Trying to Conceive
* 🪺 IVF Support Mode
* 🌸 Perimenopause Prep (future)
UI adapts guidance + dashboards accordingly.

Design vibe: Structured, analytical, fast to scan Goal: Clinical decision support, not fluff

🖥️ 1. Patient Overview Dashboard
At login, doctor sees patient tiles with risk flags:
Patient	Cycle Pattern	Genomic Risk	Current Focus
Ananya S	Short luteal	↑ PCOS PRS	TTC 6 months
Meera D	Irregular	Normal PRS	PMS severe

📊 2. Individual Patient Deep View
A. Cycle Physiology Timeline
Graph overlays:
* Cycle phase detection
* Basal temp / wearables
* Reported symptoms
* Predicted hormone curves (model-based)
Doctor sees pattern, not just dates

B. Genomic & Risk Panel

Domain	Finding	Clinical Relevance
Ovarian reserve genetics	Slight early decline tendency	Consider AMH monitoring earlier
Insulin sensitivity	Elevated risk	Screen for metabolic markers

Action buttons:
* Add note to chart
* Share simplified insight with patient
* Adjust care pathway
