Redesign and enhance the user experience of an existing clinician dashboard for Saivie Women’s Health Clinic. The platform already includes EMR, cycle intelligence, pregnancy monitoring, postpartum tracking, genomic risk data, and AI-based insights. Your goal is to make the UX faster, clearer, and more clinically actionable while keeping a calm, premium medical aesthetic.

🎯 UX GOALS

Reduce cognitive overload for doctors

Surface critical insights within 5 seconds

Make clinical decisions easier, not data heavier

Maintain emotional softness (this is women’s health, not ICU software)

🧭 GLOBAL UX IMPROVEMENTS
1️⃣ Sticky Patient Context Header

When a clinician opens a patient, a fixed header should always remain visible:

Contains:

Patient Name | Age

Life Stage Tag (Cycle / TTC / Pregnant / Postpartum)

Risk Badge (Low / Moderate / High)

Cycle Day OR Gestational Week OR Postpartum Week

Last Visit Date

Right side quick buttons:
[ Add Note ] [ Order Lab ] [ Send Insight to Patient ]

This prevents doctors from losing context while scrolling.

2️⃣ Convert Long Pages into Smart Collapsible Cards

Each major section should be a card with visual priority levels

Card types:

🔴 Critical Alert Card

🟠 Needs Attention Card

🟢 Stable Info Card

Example:
🔴 “Luteal Phase < 9 days detected”
🟠 “Sleep deficit increasing PMS severity”
🟢 “Cycle length stable for 3 months”

Doctors should immediately see what needs action vs what is informational.

3️⃣ Add a “Clinical Snapshot” Summary at Top

Before graphs and data, show a one-glance medical summary:

Reproductive Snapshot

Avg Cycle: 28d | Ovulatory: Yes

Luteal Length: 9d (borderline short)

PMS Severity: Moderate

TTC: Yes (3 months)

Genomic Risks: PCOS ↑, Thyroid ↑

This saves doctors from reading the whole chart.

🧠 REPRODUCTIVE INTELLIGENCE PANEL UX
Make Graphs Clinician-Readable, Not Decorative

Use clean medical-style line graphs

Toggle buttons: Hormones | Symptoms | Combined

Hovering over a day shows:
“Cycle Day 21 – Progesterone peak expected, pain score 6/10 logged”

Add AI Interpretation Box under each graph:

“Pain clusters in mid-luteal phase across 3 cycles. Pattern suggests progesterone sensitivity.”

This turns visuals into clinical reasoning, not just charts.

🧬 GENOMIC PANEL UX IMPROVEMENT

Instead of listing risks, use tiered cards:

PCOS Predisposition
Risk Level: High
Clinical Meaning: Higher likelihood of insulin resistance and ovulatory irregularity

Button:
[ See How This Affects Current Cycle ]

This connects static genetics → active physiology.

🤰 PREGNANCY PANEL UX
Replace scattered data with Trimester-Based Layout

Header:
Week 24 • Second Trimester

Below in 3 columns:

Maternal Trends

Weight vs expected

BP trend

Fatigue score

Fetal Indicators

Movement logs

Growth scan summary

Risk Watch

GDM risk

Pre-eclampsia risk

Anemia likelihood

AI Insight should always be in a highlighted soft panel:

“BP rising faster than baseline. Monitor weekly.”

🤱 POSTPARTUM PANEL UX

This should feel like recovery tracking, not pregnancy leftover.

Top recovery score bar:
Physical Recovery | Hormone Reset | Mood Stability | Lactation

Color coded bars show progress.

Below that:

Flag Cards
🟠 Sleep deficit worsening mood trend
🔴 Thyroid risk markers + fatigue
🟢 Pelvic recovery progressing well

📝 EMR UX IMPROVEMENT
Convert EMR tabs into timeline view

Instead of only folders, add:

Clinical Timeline

Jan 12: Severe PMS reported

Feb 02: Progesterone started

Mar 01: Symptoms improved 40%

Doctors understand progression instantly.

SOAP notes should have AI Assist button:
“Summarize last 3 months of cycle data for assessment”

🤖 AI INSIGHT UX

AI should never look like a chatbot.

Use:

Clinical Insight Box
Soft shaded panel with:

Pattern Detected
Why It Matters
Suggested Saivie Pathway

Example:

Pattern: Short luteal phase recurring
Why it matters: May reduce implantation chances
Suggested Pathway: Saivie Luteal Support Program

💬 PATIENT COMMUNICATION UX

When doctor clicks Send Insight to Patient, show preview:

Doctor View:
“Progesterone support may help luteal stability.”

Patient View Preview:
“Your body may benefit from extra hormone support after ovulation. Your Saivie doctor will guide you.”

This prevents doctors from worrying about tone.

🎨 VISUAL DESIGN RULES

Rounded cards, soft shadows

No harsh red — use muted coral for alerts

Plenty of white space

Calm, premium, intelligent

Dashboard should feel like a women’s health command center, not hospital software

⚡ PERFORMANCE UX

Dashboard must load key summary within 2 seconds

Lazy-load heavy charts

Mobile-friendly tablet mode for clinic use

FINAL DESIGN INTENT

This clinician platform should feel like:

“I understand this patient’s biology in 30 seconds, and I know exactly what to do next.”

Not cluttered. Not generic EMR.
This is Saivie’s intelligent women’s health operating system 🌸🧠
