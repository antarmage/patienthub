When a patient is created or first evaluated, you assign a:

🔹 CARE PATHWAY

This determines the entire layout of the clinician screen

🌸 1️⃣ HORMONE & CYCLE CARE PATIENT

(PMS, PCOS, irregular cycles, pain, mood)

Dashboard shows:

Cycle timeline

Hormone pattern graph

PMS severity tracking

Luteal phase length

AI hormone insights

Nutrition + psychology + lifestyle layers

This is what your current screen already reflects ✔

🌿 2️⃣ NATURAL CONCEPTION PATIENT

Now the screen changes to:

🩺 FERTILITY OPTIMIZATION DASHBOARD

Top Summary Bar

Trying for: 6 months

Ovulatory status

Luteal adequacy

Partner semen status

Main Panels
1️⃣ Ovulation Intelligence

Follicular growth history

LH surge tracking

BBT confirmation

2️⃣ Implantation Readiness

Luteal length trend

Progesterone levels

Endometrial thickness history

3️⃣ Timed Intercourse Planner

Fertile window guidance

AI suggestions based on past cycles

4️⃣ Correctable Factors Panel

Thyroid

Prolactin

Insulin resistance

BMI risk

AI Insight Example:

“Ovulation confirmed, but luteal phase borderline. Consider luteal progesterone support.”

🤰 5️⃣ PREGNANCY PATIENT WORKSPACE

Different world entirely.

Shows

GA (Gestational age)

Trimester tracker

Scan timeline

BP / sugar / weight

Risk flags (GDM, PIH, thyroid)

No cycle charts here.

🤱 6️⃣ POSTPARTUM PATIENT WORKSPACE

Focus shifts to:

Delivery details

Lactation tracker

Mood screening (PPD)

Cycle return monitoring

Contraception counseling

🧠 IMPORTANT: SAME PATIENT CAN TRANSITION

A woman may go:

Cycle Care → Natural Conception → IUI → Pregnancy → Postpartum

System should allow:
👉 Change Care Pathway
👉 Old data archived but visible

🏗️ WHAT YOU TELL YOUR DEVELOPER

“Saivie EMR must be care-pathway driven, not one fixed dashboard.
Each pathway loads a different clinical workspace UI.”
