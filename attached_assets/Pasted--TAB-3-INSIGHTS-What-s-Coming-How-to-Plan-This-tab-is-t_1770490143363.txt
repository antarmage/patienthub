🔮 TAB 3: INSIGHTS = “What’s Coming & How to Plan”

This tab is the future brain of the app.
It turns biology into prediction, timing, and life decisions.

Think of it as:

📅 “Your biological calendar + smart planning assistant”

🌙 1️⃣ CYCLE TIMELINE FORECAST (Hero Section)

A horizontal scrollable cycle strip:

Past ← Today → Future (next 10–14 days)

Each day has a soft color phase marker and micro-labels like:

🔥 High Energy

🧠 Focus Peak

🌙 Rest Window

💧 PMS Sensitive

Tap any future day → preview:

“In 3 days, progesterone rises. You may prefer slower workouts and solo work.”

This makes the app feel like a weather forecast for the body.

📈 2️⃣ UPCOMING BIOLOGY SHIFTS

Title: Next Biological Changes

Cards like:

🟣 Progesterone Rise in 4 Days
You may notice:
• Lower social drive
• Deeper sleep
• Increased hunger

🩸 Period Expected in 12 Days
Best to:
• Reduce high-stress scheduling
• Prioritize iron-rich meals

This helps women prepare, not react.

This turns the app into a life strategist, not just a tracker.
